# Threads: quick start guide

*Last updated September 26, 2026, for Threads 1.0.1.*

## What Threads is

Threads is a shared timeline for a campaign: one colored thread per party, character or strand of world events, running side by side day by day. One person keeps the shared version, usually the DM, and everyone else reads it and keeps their own notes on top.

You don't need an account or anything installed. Open the link your group shares and it works in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

**Your first five minutes**

1. Scroll the timeline sideways. Each dot is something that happened to that thread on that day.
2. Click a dot, or anywhere in a day's column. The panel below shows what every thread was doing that day.
3. Click an event in the panel to open its full details: beats, quotes and notes.
4. Type a character's name in **Follow** to light up every day they appear.
5. Choose **How this works** in the top bar any time you get stuck.

## Reading the timeline

Each row is a thread, called a lane. Days run left to right, and the day you've selected is shaded.

- **Picking a day:** click any dot, or anywhere in a day's column, including its number. **Previous** and **Next** under the panel, or the arrow keys, step between days that have something recorded.
- **The day panel:** each event shows its lane, title, place and summary. The line underneath says what's inside, such as "Show 8 beats, 3 quotes". Click the event to open it, and again to close it.
- **Show all details:** opens every event at once, and Threads remembers that choice on your device. Use it to read a whole day; leave it off to compare lanes without scrolling.
- **Lane buttons:** the colored buttons above the timeline hide and show lanes, handy for following just your party.
- **Connections:** a curved line between two lanes means they touched that day: a letter, a meeting, someone crossing over. They're listed in the panel too.
- **Shading:** a shaded stretch on a lane is a span, such as a journey, a siege or time in another realm. Hover over it, or check the legend, for its name.

**Following someone.** Type a name, place or word in **Follow**. Every day that mentions it lights up, and matches are highlighted in the panel. If the match is only inside an event's hidden details, that event opens itself and says the name is mentioned inside. For characters who travel between parties, their path is drawn across the lanes.

**Event labels.** Most events are simply confirmed. You may also see *Partly known* (pieced together later), *Placeholder* (details still to come) or *Upcoming* (hasn't happened yet).

## Who's where

Under each day's events, **Who's where** lists who was with each party that day. Lanes that don't track people, such as world events, are left out.

| You'll see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| A plain name | They were with this party the day before too |
| (joins) | New to this party today |
| (joins from The Lantern Company) | Came over from another party today or overnight |
| (rejoins) | Back with a party they were with earlier |
| Departs: Name (to The Gull Street Crew) | Left today to join another party |
| Departs: Name (dies) | Left today, with the reason the keeper gave |

Someone who switches parties partway through a day shows up in both: departing the first party and joining the second. After a party's latest day, its list is empty until the keeper moves that party's "Now at" day on.

## When time runs differently

Some spans mark a place where time doesn't match the rest of the world, such as a fey realm or a timeless court. Every event inside one shows its local time, for example "day 2 there".

- **Faster there:** several days pass there for each day here. Those days are drawn wider on the timeline, with small numbers along the lane for each local day and one spot per local day for an event.
- **Slower there:** one day there lasts several days here. Each local day is a single wide spot, and its event sits across it on a colored bar, marked "which lasts days 5 to 8 here".

The other lanes carry on normally around it, so you can see what everyone else was doing while one party was away.

## Keeping your own notes

Choose **Edit** to add or change anything. Your changes are saved in your browser as your own copy; nobody else sees them, and the shared version isn't touched.

- **Adding an event:** click an empty spot on a lane. That day is selected and the lane is highlighted in the panel below; choose **Add an event here**.
- **Changing an event:** click it to select it, then choose its **Edit** button in the panel, or double-click it on the timeline. Add beats, quotes, a place and notes; choose **Done** when finished.
- **Private notes:** a box in the editor for things only you should see, such as your character's secrets. They never leave your copy unless you check "Include private notes" when exporting.
- **Who's where:** in edit mode, click a name in the day panel to move them to another party, mark them departing (with a reason, such as "dies"), or fix their dates. Use the add box underneath to add someone; set its "from" day earlier for someone who's been there all along.
- **Undo:** in edit mode, **Undo** steps back through your changes.
- **More than one timeline:** switch between them with the timeline list in the top bar; its Manage timelines option duplicates or deletes them. **New timeline**, beside it, starts a fresh one. To rename a timeline, change its title in Timeline settings.

Because your copy lives in one browser, a different device or browser starts fresh from the shared version. Export your timeline to move it, as below.

## Sending your additions to the keeper

Wrote up your party's session, or spotted something missing? Send it to whoever keeps the shared timeline:

1. Choose **Export**, pick **This timeline**, and leave "Include private notes" unchecked.
2. Save the file. If your browser can't download it, Threads shows the text instead: copy it into a file ending in `.json`.
3. Post the file wherever your group talks, such as Discord or your play-by-post thread.

The keeper sees exactly what you added or changed and picks what to keep. Nothing of theirs is removed by your file, and your private notes are never included.

## When the shared timeline is updated

When the keeper publishes a new version, a notice appears at the top the next time you open the page. You choose what happens to your copy:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Load it, replacing my copy | Replaces your copy with the new version. Your own edits to that timeline are lost. |
| Load it as a separate copy | Adds the new version as its own timeline marked "(published)", next to your copy. |
| Keep mine | Dismisses the notice and leaves your copy as it is. |

If you've never edited anything, loading it is the easy choice. If you've added notes, load it as a separate copy, then copy over anything you still need.

If the keeper adds a whole new timeline, it simply appears in your timeline list.

## For the keeper

The keeper is whoever maintains the shared version, usually the DM. The routine is: collect players' files, bring in what you want, then publish.

**Bringing in a player's file.** Choose **Import** and pick their file, or paste its text. If it's a copy of a timeline you already have, Threads lists every new and changed entry with a checkbox and a "Show theirs" view. Check what to keep and choose **Bring in**. A merge only adds and updates: nothing of yours is deleted, their private notes are ignored, and one **Undo** takes the whole merge back. A file for a timeline you don't have is added as a new one.

**Publishing.** On a claude.ai page, choose **Publish to this page**. Everyone gets a notice offering the new version next time they open it. On a self-hosted page, replace the file on your server instead.

| To do this | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Add a missed day for one party | Edit, select the day, then **Insert a day here** in that lane's block. Only that lane's later days move. |
| Remove a day for one party | **Remove this day** in that lane's block. You're asked first if it holds an event. |
| Add or remove a day for everyone | The buttons under the timeline, marked "on every lane" |
| Move people, mark deaths and departures | Click names under Who's where in edit mode |
| Set where each party is now | Timeline settings, **Lanes** tab: "Now at day" |
| Turn Who's where on or off for a lane | Lanes tab: the "Who's here" checkbox |
| Mark another realm, or a journey or siege | Timeline settings, **Spans** tab |
| Make time run faster or slower there | Spans tab: "Time there runs" the same, faster or slower |
| Fix all of someone's stints at once | Timeline settings, **Characters** tab |

Making a span slower can put several existing events into one day there. Threads asks before combining them into one event, keeping the others as beats, and Undo reverses it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| My notes have disappeared | You're probably on a different device or browser, or in a private window. Your copy lives in the browser where you made it. Export from there and Import here. |
| I don't see the new version everyone's talking about | Refresh the page. If you chose Keep mine earlier, the notice won't reappear until the next update. |
| A party's Who's where list is empty | That day is past the party's "Now at" day. The keeper can move it on in the Lanes tab. |
| Someone shows as "joins" but was there all along | In edit mode, click their name and set an earlier "from" day. |
| I can't add a second event on a day | Each lane has one event per day, so add more detail as beats. The exception is a span where time runs faster, which has a spot per local day. |
| A span change was refused | It would have left an event without a spot. Move or delete that event first; the status line says which. |
| Export only shows a box of text | That happens where downloads aren't available. Copy all of it into a file ending in `.json`. |
| Something went wrong while editing | Choose **Undo** in edit mode, as many times as you need. |

For anything not covered here, choose **How this works** in the top bar.
